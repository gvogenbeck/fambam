Draft day

Show up ready

  1. Confirm app accessHave the Yahoo Fantasy app ready, and verify you can get into the league and see your team.
  2. Ditch the default team nameUpdate your team name and logo. Yahoo defaults are for lazy noobs.
  3. Know your settingsKnow your league settings and roster slots before the clock starts.
  4. Do your homeworkHave your research done and understand your pre-draft rankings.
  5. Ask before you arriveAsk your commissioner any questions you have — better now than mid-draft.
  6. Cash or VenmoBringing cash is fine, but you can also Venmo the commissioner your entry fee beforehand.
Words you'll hear

Glossary

ADP
Average draft position — the round and pick where a player usually goes.
Snake draft
Pick order reverses each round, so the last picker in round one picks first in round two.
Tiers
Grouping similar players so you know when a position is about to fall off a cliff.
PPR
Points per reception. A scoring style where every catch adds points on top of yardage and touchdowns — full PPR awards a full point per catch, half PPR awards half a point, and standard scoring awards none.
Flex (W/R/T)
A starting spot you can fill with a wide receiver, running back, or tight end.
Free agent
A player on no one's roster, available to add at any time — the pool everyone's pulling from on waivers.
Waivers
The claim system for unowned players. Ours process Tuesday at game time on a rolling priority list.
Injured reserve (IR)
A roster spot for players out with a qualifying injury. IR players don't count against your regular roster limit.
Handcuff
The direct backup to a starting running back you own — insurance against an injury.
Bye week
Each NFL team sits out one week. Your players score zero that week, so plan your bench.
Streaming
Swapping your defense or kicker each week to chase a favorable matchup.
Target share
The percentage of a team's passes thrown at one receiver. High share equals reliable points.
